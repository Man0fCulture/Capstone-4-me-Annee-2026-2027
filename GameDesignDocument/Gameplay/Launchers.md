# Launchers

The launcher mechanic unlocks when the player first enters the second level. Rising Slash is granted for free and equipped by default for sword mode. Other launchers can be purchased from Emmanuel from this point onwards.

A launcher can be triggered only on the ground during an ongoing combo, after a beginner or follower. It is unavailable from idle, during a finisher or after a finisher.

The input is the movement stick directed away from the active target, combined with Square or Triangle. This direction uses the position of the manually or automatically selected target.

Depending on the equipped move, a launcher sends only the enemy into the air or takes Natanael up with it. A launcher resets the index to 0.
