# Weapon 1 + Weapon 2 Special Move

The player buys the special-move slot from Emmanuel. Initial playtest price: 2,500 currency units.

The special consists of a follower followed by a finisher, configured separately from the normal combo. It has a ground configuration and an aerial configuration using aerial moves.

It is available after a beginner or follower in an ongoing combo, and unavailable from idle or after a finisher. Pressing Square + Triangle simultaneously triggers the sequence. Its finisher resets the index to 0.

The cooldown lasts 5 seconds and is shared between ground and aerial configurations. It starts when the special is activated and continues if a dodge interrupts the move. An indicator displays readiness and recharge progress.

Ground configuration examples: Advancing Thrust → Focused Volley, or Piercing Shot → Heavy Strike. These examples use catalogue moves and can be changed by the player.
