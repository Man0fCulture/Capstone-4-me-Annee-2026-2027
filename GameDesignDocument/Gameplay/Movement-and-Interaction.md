# Movement and Interaction

## Movement

On the ground, Natanael moves freely within accessible areas and can jump. Initial playtest controls: left stick for movement and right stick to rotate the camera.

In the air, the stick alone does not move Natanael. Aerial attacks and dodges provide movement towards a target. Some moves also change the player's height.

Ground speed, jump height, attack movement and dodge distances will be tuned during prototyping.

## Interaction

Near Emmanuel, the player can start a short conversation to accept or complete a mission and access the shop.

Collection and delivery crates are stationary objects. Interacting with a crate completes the corresponding mission step; the mission state represents the goods being collected or delivered.

A menu allows the player to configure combat using owned moves. Configuration is available only outside combat. Purchases take place at Emmanuel's shop, and re-equipping is free.

The button used to interact with a character or crate will be selected when the interfaces are integrated.
