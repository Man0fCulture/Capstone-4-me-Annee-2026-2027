# Pistoblade

The sword and pistol are two offensive branches of the Pistoblade. The pistol has its own combo moves and finishers. Its range remains short, with slightly more reach than the sword.

Players choose a move for its purpose: concentrated damage, area coverage, movement towards a target, range or an effect on the enemy. Pistol attacks contribute to both combo construction and style gain.
