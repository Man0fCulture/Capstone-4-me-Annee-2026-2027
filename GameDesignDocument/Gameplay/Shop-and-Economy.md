# Shop and Economy

Emmanuel's shop is in the first level. It sells moves, the fourth ground slot and the special-move slot. Additional launchers become purchasable from the second level.

A purchase permanently unlocks the item in the current progression. Removing a move from a combo does not remove ownership. Acquired moves and slots are recorded in saves; loading a checkpoint restores purchases present in that saved state.

Combos can be reconfigured for free at Emmanuel's shop or through a menu outside combat.

## Initial playtest values

| Item                            | Initial playtest amount |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Main mission reward             | 1,000                   |
| Hunt reward                     | 500                     |
| Additional beginner or follower | 500                     |
| Additional finisher or launcher | 800                     |
| Fourth ground slot              | 2,000                   |
| Special-move slot               | 2,500                   |

The currency has no name yet. Prices and rewards provide an initial balance scale.

The fourth ground slot and the two additional ground followers cost 3,000 units together. Adding the special-move slot brings this set to 5,500 units. Players choose between extending combos, obtaining the special and buying variants.

At these initial reward values, the first three main missions grant 3,000 units. Completing those missions and all six hunts once would grant 6,000 units; the hunt targets and objectives remain to be defined.

Repeatable hunts should fund optional purchases. Their duration will inform reward tuning.
