# Move Catalogue

21 planned moves: twelve owned at the start, Rising Slash granted in level 2, and the others purchased from Emmanuel. Additional variants will be selected for production during prototyping.

## Ground moves

| Mode | Category | Move | Purpose | Availability |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sword | Beginner | Swift Slash | Fast close-range hit | Starting move |
| Sword | Follower | Side Sweep | Arc-shaped slash hitting nearby enemies | Starting move |
| Sword | Follower | Advancing Thrust | Move towards the target, then strike with the blade's tip | Shop |
| Sword | Finisher | Heavy Strike | Concentrated close-range damage | Starting move |
| Pistol | Beginner | Direct Shot | Targeted short-range shot | Starting move |
| Pistol | Follower | Piercing Shot | Pass through aligned enemies | Starting move |
| Pistol | Follower | Fan Volley | Shots spread in front of the player; each target receives part of the volley | Shop |
| Pistol | Finisher | Focused Volley | Several shots at one nearby target | Starting move |
| Pistol | Finisher | Circular Volley | Rotate and fire through 360° at nearby enemies | Shop |

Advancing Thrust and Fan Volley provide a second ground follower for each mode after buying the fourth slot.

## Aerial moves

| Mode | Category | Move | Purpose | Availability |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sword | Beginner | Aerial Slash | Short approach towards the target followed by a strike | Starting move |
| Sword | Follower | Aerial Thrust | Reach a more distant target and strike | Starting move |
| Sword | Finisher | Plunging Strike | Bring the player and target to the ground | Starting move |
| Sword | Finisher | Suspended Slash | Wide strike; the player remains airborne afterwards | Shop |
| Pistol | Beginner | Aerial Shot | Targeted shot from the air | Starting move |
| Pistol | Follower | Pass-through Shot | Pass through nearby aligned targets | Starting move |
| Pistol | Finisher | Downward Shot | Bring the target to the ground while the player remains airborne | Starting move |
| Pistol | Finisher | Aerial Burst | Concentrated fire on a nearby target; the player remains airborne | Shop |

## Ground launchers

| Mode | Move | Effect | Availability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sword | Rising Slash | Launch the target while the player stays on the ground | Granted in level 2 |
| Sword | Ascension | Launch the target and take the player upwards with it | Purchase from level 2 |
| Pistol | Launch Shot | Launch a short-range target while the player stays on the ground | Purchase from level 2 |
| Pistol | Orbital Shot | Launch the target and leave a circular trail in the air | Purchase from level 2 |

Orbital Shot's trail lasts 2 seconds. It damages the target and resets its fall at every damage application. Damage values and application frequency will be tuned during prototyping.
