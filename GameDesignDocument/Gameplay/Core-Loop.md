# Core Experience and Gameplay Loop

Combat should feel fast and reward the player's choice of moves. Each attack input selects the sword or pistol mode at a shared stage of the combo. Movement built into attacks, launchers and finishers gives these choices a purpose.

The main loop is:

1. Explore Markyo's districts and accept a mission.
2. Reach the objective and fight enemies.
3. Vary moves, weapon modes and ground/aerial attacks to build style.
4. Use attack effects and stagger to continue fighting.
5. Complete the mission and receive money.
6. Buy moves or a slot from Emmanuel, then configure combos for the next encounters.

Missions guide travel through two connected levels. The player returns to the shop between main missions.
