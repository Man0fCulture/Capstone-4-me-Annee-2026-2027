# Development Beyond the PoC

- Combo capacity up to 8 slots: 1 beginner, up to 6 distinct followers and 1 finisher.
- Separate purchases for additional ground and aerial slots, with optional activation.
- A broader move catalogue and bestiary after validating combat and production workload.
- Parrying, an independent support shot and additional directional inputs to investigate.
- Console ports to investigate after the PC version.

Prices for slots 4–8 remain to be tuned. These extensions are planned separately from the PoC baseline.
