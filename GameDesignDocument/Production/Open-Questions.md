# Details to Define During Development

| Topic | Remaining design work |
|---|---|
| Enemies | Distinct attacks, launch resistances and stagger reactions |
| Hunts | Define the six contracts once enemy variety is sufficient |
| Balance | HP, damage, reach, attack cadence, rewards and prices |
| Style | Rank thresholds, gains, repetition penalties, decay and bonuses |
| Stagger | Gain per hit and stun duration for each enemy |
| Combos | Interval between attacks, when its timer starts and input buffering |
| Orbital Shot | Damage and application frequency during the 2-second trail |
| Dodge | Distance, direction, invulnerability timing and aerial falling |
| Targeting and camera | Ranges, target loss, obstacles and framing settings |
| Levels and missions | Layout, encounters, access, duration and transitions |
| Interfaces | Interaction button, screens and visual feedback |
| Production | Engine, asset workflow and selection of variants to produce |

The earlier idea of an enemy becoming more aggressive at 50% stagger, announced by a shout, remains a hypothesis to test during enemy design.
