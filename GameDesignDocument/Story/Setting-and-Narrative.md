# Markyo — Setting and Narrative

Natanael arrives in Markyo looking for work and money. He meets Emmanuel, an Indian grocer and engineer who designed the Pistoblade. Emmanuel owes money to Steezy, a local crime boss.

Natanael takes missions to help Emmanuel and fund his weapon's development. Debt repayment advances through the missions as part of the story. The player's rewards are used to buy moves and slots.

In the fourth mission, Steezy stops negotiating and wants Emmanuel killed. Natanael fights an armoured samurai serving Steezy, the final boss of the PoC.
