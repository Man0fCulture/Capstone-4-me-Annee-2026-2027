# Chapter 1 — Emmanuel and Steezy

Natanael arrives in Markyo and accepts Emmanuel's jobs. The first missions lead him from the shop's storage area to the harbour, where he collects and delivers goods intended to finance debt repayment.

The sale succeeds, but Steezy threatens Emmanuel and sends an armoured samurai to kill him. Repayment stops in the story at mission 4; Natanael confronts this man in the boss arena.
