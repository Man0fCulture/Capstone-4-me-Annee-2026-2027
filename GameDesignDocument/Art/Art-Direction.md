# Art Direction

Markyo combines bright Mediterranean façades, pale stone, warm roofs and sea blue with vertical signs and Japanese technological details.

The game uses low-poly models and cel-shading. Large surfaces use Mediterranean colours; neon accents appear on signs and technological elements. The 3D models are created for the project.

| Colour | Hex code | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Pale stone | #F2E3C6 | Façades and floors |
| Sea blue | #237FBA | Sea and shutters |
| Terracotta | #C96A4A | Walls and roofs |
| Ink blue | #1B263B | Shadows and metal |
| Sun yellow | #F2BC57 | Light and details |
| Neon pink | #E84B9A | Signs and technology |

Each colour has shadow, base and light variants for cel-shading.
