# Planned Playable Content

The [[GameDesignDocument/Planning/PoC|16 December PoC]] uses four produced attacks with reused animations, one functional mob whose type remains to be selected, one short mission and one test hunt. The alpha adds the two complete levels, four main missions and three selected enemy types. Distinct moves are produced across later milestones.

| Content or system | Selected scope |
|---|---|
| World | 2 connected explorable levels and 1 boss arena |
| Main missions | 4 missions, ending with the boss fight in mission 4 |
| Side missions | 6 repeatable hunt contracts planned; their detailed design is deferred until enemy variety is sufficient |
| Bestiary | Rats, Cerberus and an armoured samurai |
| Playable character | Natanael |
| Normal combat | Sword and pistol modes with a shared combo index |
| Ground combo | 3 starting slots, with an optional purchasable fourth slot |
| Aerial combo | 3 slots, configured separately from ground combos |
| Launcher | Separate move category, available in level 2 |
| Special move | Follower followed by finisher, available after purchasing its slot |
| Defence | Ground and aerial dodge |
| Progression | Mission rewards, shop, permanent purchases and free configuration |
| Saving | Player, world and mission state recorded at checkpoints |

Progression up to 8 slots and additional aerial slots are options for development beyond the PoC. Parrying and a possible independent support shot remain research ideas.

## World organisation

| Area | Purpose in the PoC |
|---|---|
| First level | Emmanuel's shop, storage yard and first encounters; return point for completing main missions |
| Second level | Harbour district, mission 2 depot and mission 3 delivery point |
| Boss arena | Mission 4 fight against the armoured samurai |

The two levels are connected and explorable. Their layouts, travel distances and expected traversal times will be developed during level design.
