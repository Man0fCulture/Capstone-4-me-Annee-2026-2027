# PoC Missions

The PoC contains four main missions across two connected levels and a boss arena. Missions 1–3 introduce Markyo and advance Emmanuel's repayment in the story; mission 4 pits Natanael against Steezy's samurai.

## Main missions

- [[GameDesignDocument/Missions/Mission-1-Rats-on-Credit|Mission 1 — Rats on Credit]]
- [[GameDesignDocument/Missions/Mission-2-The-Barking-Package|Mission 2 — The Barking Package]]
- [[GameDesignDocument/Missions/Mission-3-The-Last-Delivery|Mission 3 — The Last Delivery]]
- [[GameDesignDocument/Missions/Mission-4-Steezys-Samurai|Mission 4 — Steezy's Samurai]]

Missions follow this narrative sequence. Exact access conditions, transitions to the arena and target durations will be specified during integration. Customers and suppliers can be mentioned in Emmanuel's dialogue.

## Side missions

[[GameDesignDocument/Missions/Hunts|Repeatable hunts]]: six repeatable contracts are planned. Their detailed design will take place when the bestiary provides sufficient variety.
