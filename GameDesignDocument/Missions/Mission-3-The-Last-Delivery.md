# Mission 3 — The Last Delivery

Emmanuel has found a buyer for the goods and hopes he can finally breathe again. Rats and a Cerberus occupy the delivery point.

The player reaches another part of the harbour, defeats the enemies, interacts with the delivery crate and returns to the shop. Delivery and the report complete the mission. The sale succeeds, but Steezy threatens Emmanuel: “Keep your money. It's your head I want now.”
