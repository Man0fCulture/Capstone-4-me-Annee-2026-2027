# Mission 2 — The Barking Package

Goods already ordered by Emmanuel are waiting at the harbour depot. He intends to sell them to repay another part of his debt, but a Cerberus has made the depot its lair.

The player accepts the mission, enters the second level, defeats Cerberus and interacts with the crate before returning to Emmanuel. The mission succeeds when Cerberus is defeated, the crate collected and the report completed. Entering level 2 for the first time unlocks Rising Slash.
