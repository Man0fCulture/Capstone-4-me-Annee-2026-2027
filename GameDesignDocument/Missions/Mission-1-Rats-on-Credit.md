# Mission 1 — Rats on Credit

Natanael is looking for his first job. Rats occupy Emmanuel's storage area and prevent him from fulfilling an order. Emmanuel lends him the Pistoblade to clear the yard: “My customers buy on credit. The rats help themselves.”

The player talks to Emmanuel, visits the storage area in the first level, eliminates the rats and returns to the shop. The mission succeeds when the rats are defeated and Emmanuel receives the report. The order can be sent out and the first repayment advances in the story.
