# Mission 4 — Steezy's Samurai

The threat leads to a fight in the boss arena. Steezy wants Emmanuel killed and sends his armoured samurai. Natanael must defeat this opponent to prevent Emmanuel's death. This mission ends the PoC.

Debt repayment stops at this point in the story. The fight does not depend on paying a specified debt amount.
