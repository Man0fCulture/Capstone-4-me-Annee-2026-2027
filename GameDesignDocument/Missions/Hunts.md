# Repeatable Hunts

Six hunt contracts are planned across the two levels. Each asks the player to defeat a target or group of enemies in an area.

The player accepts a contract, eliminates its targets and automatically receives the reward. Accepting the contract again resets its targets and allows unlimited replay.

Detailed design of the six hunts is deferred until the bestiary provides sufficient variety. Targets, quantities, locations and access conditions will be selected at that stage.
