# FranceTokyo — Overview

| Element | Design intention |
|---|---|
| Genre | 3D hack'n'slash, action-RPG and exploration |
| Setting | Markyo, a fictional region combining Marseille and Tokyo |
| Core experience | Build fast combos by alternating between two modes of the same weapon |
| Player character | Natanael, a young man who wants to become a rōnin and make his fortune |
| Weapon | The Pistoblade, a gunblade with sword and pistol modes |
| Art direction | Low poly and cel-shading; a Mediterranean city with Japanese and futuristic details |
| Audience and tone | Teenagers and players who enjoy combo-based combat; everyday humour and serious story stakes |
| Initial platform | PC, played with a controller |
| Possible later releases | Console versions to investigate after validating the PC game |
