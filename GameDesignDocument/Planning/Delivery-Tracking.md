# Delivery Tracking

For each delivery, retain:

- The build, its version and instructions to run it.
- Selected objectives and their completion status.
- Evidence of completion criteria: reports, screenshots or videos.
- Bugs, limitations and remaining work.
- A post-mortem: what worked, what caused problems, lessons learned and adjustments for the next stage.
