# Production Baseline

## PoC — 16 December

The first delivery proves the gameplay loop with a small production budget:

- Four attacks to produce: one sword beginner, one sword follower, one sword finisher and one pistol shot reused across combo roles.
- Reused animations for aerial combat, one test launcher and the configurable special.
- Three ground slots and three aerial slots for each mode.
- One functional mob, whose type remains to be selected, with at least one attack; one passive resistant target for stagger tests.
- One blockout combat area, Emmanuel's shop space and a test zone transition.
- One short mission and one repeatable test hunt using the same mob.
- Rewards, special-slot purchase, outside-combat configuration, checkpoints and Game Over.
- Temporary interface, effects and audio where needed.

Completion criteria: [[GameDesignDocument/Planning/PoC|PoC — 16 December 2026]].

## Planned playable content across later milestones

The broader selected content remains the target for the later builds:

- Two connected levels and a boss arena.
- Four main missions, including the three selected stories and the final fight.
- Three enemy types: rats, Cerberus and an armoured samurai.
- Twelve starting moves: beginner, follower and finisher for each mode on the ground and in the air.
- Three ground and aerial slots; an optional purchasable fourth ground slot.
- Shared index, configuration outside combat, launchers, special move, dodge, style and stagger.
- Emmanuel's shop, rewards, player/world saves and Game Over.
- Low-poly and cel-shaded art using the Marseille + Tokyo palette.

The alpha integrates the two complete levels, arena, four main missions and three selected enemy types. Distinct starting moves and additional variants are produced across later milestones, replacing the prototype reuse.

Advancing Thrust and Fan Volley are the two followers needed to test the fourth ground slot when that content is produced. The special reuses owned moves. Selection of the three additional finisher variants for production remains open.

## Deferred content

The December PoC tests one repeatable contract for acceptance, rewards and target resets. The six hunts will be designed once enemy variety is sufficient. Their targets, objectives and delivery dates remain to be defined.

The December PoC requires one functional mob with at least one attack; its type remains to be selected. Later builds introduce the three selected types and their own attacks; full patterns will be defined during enemy design.

Progression up to 8 slots, additional aerial slots, parrying and console ports remain extensions to plan separately.
