# Beta — 24 March 2027

**Objective:** stabilise the route and integrate visual presentation, audio and combat feedback.

| Work | Expected output | Completion criterion | Quantity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Art direction | Environments and characters using the selected look | Areas use low poly, cel-shading and the palette; combat remains readable | 2 levels and 1 arena; Natanael, Emmanuel and 3 enemy types |
| Audio and effects | Integrated sound effects and visual feedback | Impacts, shots, dodges and important states are recognisable; the explosion effect and range bonus communicate style | Feedback for the produced actions |
| Interface | Integrated system presentation | HP, style, target, special, objectives, shop and configuration are usable with a controller | GDD screens and indicators |
| Stability | Tested route and tracked bugs | A new game reaches the end of the PoC; Game Over, resuming and zone transitions do not block missions | At least 1 recorded complete playthrough, including death and resume |
| Performance | Measurements on a documented reference PC | Measurements are retained and the route's main slowdowns are identified and corrected | Measurements in both levels, the arena and combat scenes |

**Expected delivery:** beta build, playtest summary, remaining bugs and limitations, performance measurements and a post-mortem.

**Dependencies and risks:** availability of models, animations, effects and audio; camera readability; fixes that affect saves or missions. The reference PC and performance targets will be chosen after initial measurements.

**Integration and fixes:** last 7 days for stabilisation and testing, with this build's content frozen before that period.
