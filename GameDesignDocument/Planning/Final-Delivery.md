# Final Delivery — 26 May 2027

**Objective:** deliver a standalone, stable PC version of the selected scope with its documentation.

| Work | Expected output | Completion criterion | Quantity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Delivered game | PC build with documented controls | The build starts without the editor and the main route can be completed from the start to the boss | 2 levels, 1 arena, 4 missions and 3 enemy types |
| Progression verification | Tested purchases, configuration and saving | The verification scenario is executed; results and limitations are recorded | 1 complete verification scenario |
| Additional content | Exact inventory of integrated moves and contracts | The GDD matches the build; the status of all 6 hunts and move variants is explicit | Final inventory of content actually delivered |
| Documentation and review | Updated GDD, launch guide, known bugs and post-mortem | An outside reader can run the build, play and compare results against the selected objectives | 1 build, documentation and final review |

**Dependencies and risks:** regressions after final fixes, build preparation and agreement between the delivered game and objectives. Extensions beyond the PoC will be added to this plan only after defining their content and workload.

**Integration and fixes:** last 14 days for testing, fixes, delivery preparation and the review.
